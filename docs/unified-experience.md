# Navigation and interaction design

Footie has four primary destinations: **Match Centre**, **Highlights**, **Clubhouse** and **Footie**. Match Centre is the default. Analyst Mode opens from its full-match view. The retired Tools and Studio interfaces are not part of public navigation.

## Connected journeys

- Match Centre links a match overview, filtered moments, shot map and continuous replay. Saving a moment creates a reference in Highlights.
- Highlights keeps the replay, Footie's Summary, annotations and notes together. Preloaded highlights also offer Breakdown; changing between Watch and Breakdown preserves the selected highlight.
- Clubhouse connects player and club records, follows, comparisons and match history. Contextual Ask Footie supplies the relevant subject and season.
- Footie's contextual drawer preserves the source page. Analyst Mode instead keeps its own ongoing specialist conversation beside the match experience.

## Shared presentation

Cream surfaces, forest-green text, sage borders and restrained gold accents provide a shared theme. Club identities add crests and accents; pitch colours distinguish teams. Desktop provides space for the pitch and analyst desks, while narrow layouts use compact controls and progressive disclosure.

## State and accessibility

Hash routes support browser back/forward. Legacy `#matchday` and `#explore` links retain compatibility with highlight views. Favourites and saved highlights are device-local. Match changes, seeks and corrections invalidate stale analysis; evidence remains pinned to its source clock.

Player hover/focus cards reveal names and roles without making moving markers perform an unrelated click action. Drawing pauses the highlight. Reduced-motion preferences and keyboard focus are retained. See [Highlights](highlights-workspace.md), [Analyst Mode](analyst-mode.md) and the [walkthrough](demo-guide.md).
