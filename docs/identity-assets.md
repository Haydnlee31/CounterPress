# Matchday identity and asset provenance

Matchday uses original fictional display identities. They label synthetic event
actors; they do not change stable IDs, calculations or evidence. Any resemblance
to a real person or club is incidental. This catalogue is not a trademark clearance.

The monograms are plain letters rendered with original CSS. The pitch is original
SVG geometry in metres. There are no imported crests, kits, sponsor marks, player
photographs, broadcast footage, cloned voices, or third-party football datasets.
Fonts use the local system font stack. Screenshots show CounterPress's synthetic
interface and were captured from the running app.

Real clubs and players remain outside the release. Adding them requires a separate
asset/source register and documented permissions covering the hackathon's rules,
submission, video and promotional licence. A disclaimer alone is not clearance.
