# Matchday formative fan test

Status: **prepared, not conducted**. Five real participants are needed. Automated
browser tests are separate evidence and must not be entered as participant results.
Use the live Matchday release before adding more features. Allow 10–12 minutes per
person; ask permission before any recording. Do not collect names, contact details,
account information or unnecessary personal data. Label notes P01–P05.

Recruit football fans with different levels of tactical knowledge. Include mobile
users and, if available, English and Simplified Chinese readers. This convenience
sample is formative feedback, not representative research or a statistically
powered comparison. No participant has been recruited or contacted by the app.

## Moderator preparation

Open the [live demo](https://counterpress.icyplant-0eb80d8e.eastus.azurecontainerapps.io/)
in a fresh browser profile, allow the app to wake, and clear saved preferences.
Time the warm experience separately from cold startup. Keep the participant on
Matchday. Do not name a button or demonstrate the interaction before the task.
Ask them to think aloud; record where they hesitate and any help you give.
Never suggest that this fictional match is a real fixture.

Say: “We are testing this prototype, not you. Please try each task as you normally
would. You can stop at any time. Tell me what you expect to happen.”

## Tasks and scoring

| Task to read aloud | Observe without coaching | Success criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You stepped away from the match. Find out what changed and tell a friend in one sentence. | First useful action, seconds to explanation, whether they seek a tutorial. | Within 30 seconds, describes the increase in recorded high recoveries from 1 to 4 across two five-minute windows. Does not describe a win probability or proof of tactical superiority. |
| You are following Northport. Make this view suit you, then reload it. | Finds following controls, notices story priority/accent, understands persistence. | Choice survives reload; can explain that the underlying match facts did not change. |
| You are unsure about a claim. Find an event behind it and tell me who was involved. | Finds readable evidence; distinguishes a location illustration from footage. | Identifies a named actor/event and understands it supports the selected comparison. No JSON knowledge required. |
| Explore the story about corrections. Compare the original story with its later versions. | Finds the update action/replay; response to withdrawal and replacement stories. | Explains that later evidence changed the recovery story, notices withdrawal, and can return to the original time. Help to reach the scenario is logged separately. |
| Return to a neutral view and choose the language you prefer. | Can clear following and find language controls without losing trust in the facts. | Clears device preferences; switches language and understands this is the same synthetic data. |

For each task record unassisted / assisted / unsuccessful, elapsed seconds,
misunderstanding, exact short quote (with consent), and severity. Separate product
confusion from network delay. A task requiring moderator instruction is assisted,
even if the participant eventually finishes it.

## Debrief questions

1. What is this product for, in your own words? Is the match real or simulated?
2. When would you use it while watching football? What would you expect next?
3. What does following a team change? Did anything feel misleading or confusing?
4. Did the correction increase or reduce your trust? Why?
5. Rate usefulness, clarity and trust from 1–5, then explain each rating.
6. What single change would make you more likely to use it again?

## Decision gate

Target at least 4/5 unassisted first-story explanations within 30 seconds, 4/5
finding readable evidence, and 5/5 understanding that this is synthetic data.
Treat a repeated misconception about counts, causality or withdrawn evidence as a
release-blocking content problem. These are product targets, not observed results.
For five participants, report counts and individual timings rather than a population
percentage or significance claim. If a task fails, fix the specific obstacle and
retest it with new people where possible. Do not silently delete failed sessions.

Use [the session form](fan-session-form.md). Results are intentionally blank.
The next decision is whether comprehension problems need correction before adding
hosted editorial features; more agents cannot resolve an unclear interaction.
